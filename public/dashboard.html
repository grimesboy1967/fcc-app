🔴 Critical Issues — Act Now
1. Title 4 Solutions — $50,000 loan, $29/month payment
URGENT

At $29/month on a $50,000 loan, you are paying roughly $417/month in interest alone — meaning almost none of your $29 minimum goes toward the actual balance. At this rate the loan would take over 100 years to repay. This appears to be either a balloon note, a settlement agreement, or a predatory product. Call the lender immediately and ask for a full payoff statement and amortization schedule. You need to know the true interest rate and maturity date before your next payment.

2. Seven Buy-Now-Pay-Later / Rent-to-Own accounts running simultaneously
HIGH

Afterpay, Zip (Jen), Zip (Scott), Acima, Rent-A-Center, Leaseville, and Katapult are all active at the same time, costing an estimated $380+/month in combined payments. These services make purchases feel affordable in the moment but fragment your cash flow into dozens of small obligations that are very hard to track — as your spreadsheet shows. BNPL services often carry effective APRs of 20–36% when fees are factored in. Stop using all of them immediately. Pay off the lowest balances first to eliminate accounts, then roll those payments forward.

3. Only ~$1,014/month left after all fixed obligations
HIGH

After all bills and minimum debt payments, approximately $1,014/month remains for everything else — groceries, gas, clothing, medical copays, household supplies, entertainment. Your spreadsheet shows grocery budgets of $100–150/week and entertainment of up to $275/week, which alone exceeds what's available. The math does not work at current spending levels. Any month where discretionary spending exceeds $1,014 means going deeper into debt.
